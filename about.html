ABOUT REMARKET

A simple way to reuse what already exists.

ReMarket is a second-hand product marketplace where people can discover, buy and sell pre-owned products.

OUR PURPOSE

Make second-hand shopping easier.

Instead of unused products sitting at home, ReMarket gives them an opportunity to reach someone who needs them.
